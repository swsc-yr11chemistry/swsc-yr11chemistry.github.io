How conversion works

What a prefix does

In the warm up you saw that each step down the ladder made the number 1000 times bigger. That works because a prefix is a short word at the front of a unit that tells us how big the unit is. The prefix kilo means 1000, so a kilogram is 1000 grams. The prefix milli means one thousandth, so a milligram is one thousandth of a gram.

PrefixSymbolSize compared with the base unit
gigaG1 000 000 000 times bigger (10⁹)
megaM1 000 000 times bigger (10⁶)
kilok1000 times bigger (10³)
millimone thousandth (10⁻³)
microµone millionth (10⁻⁶)
nanonone billionth (10⁻⁹)

Quick check. How many milligrams are in 1 kg of flour? Use the table to decide.

Show the answer
1 kg = 1000 g and 1 g = 1000 mg, so 1 kg = 1000 × 1000 = 1 000 000 mg.

Converting with a conversion factor

You might be tempted to move a decimal point and hope for the best. A safer method is to multiply by a conversion factor. A conversion factor is a fraction with the same amount on the top and the bottom, so it equals one. Multiplying by one does not change the amount, only the units it is written in.

Convert 2.5 kg to g.2.5 kg × 1000 g1 kg = 2500 gWe put kg on the bottom so it cancels with the kg we started with. That leaves g.
Convert 450 mg to g.450 mg × 1 g1000 mg = 0.45 gThis time mg is on the bottom, so it cancels.

Your turn. Convert 3600 mL to L.

Worked answer
3600 mL × 1 L1000 mL = 3.6 L

Does the answer make sense?

The most common mistake is putting the factor upside down. You can catch it by checking the size. A smaller unit fits into the amount more times, so the number must get bigger. A bigger unit fits fewer times, so the number must get smaller.

Quick check. Which answer makes sense for 0.8 L written in mL?

Worked answer
0.8 L × 1000 mL1 L = 800 mL. It is less than 1 L, so it must be less than 1000 mL.

Volume units: L, mL, cm³ and m³

Volumes are measured in several units. A cube with sides of 1 cm has a volume of 1 cm³, and it holds exactly 1 mL of liquid, so 1 cm³ and 1 mL are the same. A cube with sides of 1 m holds 1000 L, which is the size of a small swimming pool for one person. That is why a pool volume is often given in m³ but filled by the litre.

Watch out. 1 m³ is 1000 L, not 1000 mL. To get mL you multiply by 1000 once more, so 1 m³ = 1 000 000 mL.

Quick check. A tank holds 2.5 m³. How many litres is that?

Worked answer
2.5 m³ × 1000 L1 m³ = 2500 L

Two-step conversions and very small numbers

Some conversions skip a step on the ladder. When that happens, multiply by one conversion factor at a time. Each factor cancels one unit, so you can see the path from the starting unit to the one you want. Very small answers are easier to read in scientific notation, which writes a number as a value between 1 and 10 times a power of ten.

Convert 45 mg to kg.45 mg × 1 g1000 mg × 1 kg1000 g = 0.000 045 kg0.000 045 kg = 4.5 × 10⁻⁵ kgThe first factor cancels mg and leaves g. The second cancels g and leaves kg. The answer is small because a kilogram is a very large unit.
